05 Process

From idea to live.

    Discover

    Scope, requirements and an honest timeline. We agree on what success looks like before writing code.

    Design

    Interface and flow in Figma, mobile-first from the start — because that is how Somalia browses.

  3. 03

    Build

    Django and Flutter, tested and debugged with you before anything goes near production.

  4. 04

    Ship & support

    Deploy, monitor, patch and iterate. The relationship does not end at launch.
